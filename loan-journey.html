How it started

Right now, getting a loan means lots of back-and-forth with an Expert Loan Consultant: sharing details, uploading docs, waiting for checks, and sometimes redoing it all. It's a bit of a manual maze!

We're flipping the script: users will fill out info and upload documents themselves, and voilà, the app zooms straight to the Bank WIP stage automatically. Smooth, simple, and totally DIY!

Project overview

The education loan journey today feels like a manual obstacle course: constant back-and-forth with consultants, repeated document sharing, long wait times, and frequent rework. Instead of a smooth digital flow, users navigate a fragmented process that slows them down, increases dependency on human support, and leads to avoidable drop-offs. The system isn't scaling with user expectations for a simple, self-serve loan experience.

Tentative impact

  • Reduced ELC bandwidth usage → increased scale
  • Faster average TAT from LE to WIP
  • Consistent experience, even during off-hours
  • Stronger case for tech-led journey when showcasing to partners
  • Measurable improvement in ops accuracy due to fewer manual touchpoints

What next (Version 2)

Automate document upload and verification

To increase Auto Journey adoption, I introduced a prominent top-of-page banner that nudges users to take the next step immediately after viewing their loan offer.

After selection of lenders, users are redirected to the document upload screen.

Steps with system indicator

  1. Select the required document type and upload the file
  2. The uploaded document is being reviewed (AI or manual verification)
  3. The document has been successfully verified
  4. The system could not clearly identify or classify the document
  5. The document was rejected due to quality or clarity issues

Here, if a document is not recognized by AI but is good in quality, it gives an option to the user to self-identify.

After document submission the user can submit the application, and next the ELC will take action on the application.

Trade-off: Speed vs. Accuracy

Status of application on ELC dashboard

  1. Status of application and document status get updated on the ELC's dashboard and they can submit this application. Once submitted, it is logged into the bank account.
